About

Hi, I'm Moi — a violinist and independent music composer from Mexico, with classical and contemporary training and a Master of Arts in music. Every piece I write is a way of telling a story.

I started out producing music and audio for advertising agencies and local brands, handling the whole process from recording to post-production. Then I spent three years composing and producing the soundtrack of Bō: Path of the Teal Lotus — my debut in games, and the dream of seeing my music on Nintendo and PlayStation come true.

I like fast tempos and big percussion in a boss fight, motifs that mean something to the lore, and enough counter-melodies to keep you listening on loop. When I'm not scoring, I play violin at events and ceremonies.

Bored?

Three tiny toys, one synth.

No samples, no downloads — everything you hear is made by your browser's own oscillators, tuned to the pentatonic scales Moi writes with. Headphones on.

paper beats

A 16-step chiptune sequencer: five pentatonic rows on top, hats, snare and kick below. Tap squares, press play, and share your loop with a link.

composer's ear

Two notes, one question: what's the interval? Ten rounds, three difficulties, and a rank at the end — from listener to OST composer.

pentatonic pong

Pong, except every paddle hit plays the next note of a melody. Keep the rally alive to hear the whole phrase. First to seven.
